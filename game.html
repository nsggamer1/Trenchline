Teamwork energy

Neither player can earn much energy alone. Each panel shows how much is flowing in from your teammate.

  • The Builder earns energy whenever the Commander's units deal damage or score kills.
  • The Commander earns energy whenever the Builder's towers deal damage or score kills, plus a little for every road piece laid.

Builder: roads first

The map starts with no lanes. You get 3-block road pieces in a random queue. Each piece must touch your castle or your road.

  • After the first fork, lemonade rivers split the map into three sections. Each section leads to exactly one Old Grumpstump.
  • Your Commander can only send squads to a section your road reaches.
  • The mode key switches between Road, Towers and Cards. In Road mode, the cycle key rotates the piece.

Friendslop comms (mouse)

  • Middle-click opens a ping wheel. Commanders ask for specific towers. Builders call out power-ups, and the wheel only lists the ones ready right now.
  • Hold Alt and move the mouse to scribble on the map. Only your own team sees it.
  • Right-click the map to chat. Enter sends to your team and Shift+Enter sends to everyone.
  • CPU teammates follow your pings and chat (try "build", "road" or "push"), and they ping you back. Prank Call and Forged Treasure Map cards plant fake pings and doodles on the enemy's map.

Lanes, towers & jungle

  • Each lane has two team towers. A weak Sugar Spire sits near mid and a strong Candy Keep sits near home. A keep is immune until its spire falls. Your castle is immune until any one of your keeps falls.
  • Breaking a spire, keep or Grumpstump sends a golden power wave down that lane for the attackers.
  • Jungle dens sit in your back corners: a Gumdrop Golem and a Cotton Wisp nest. Build a road to one, then the Commander points at it to send squads straight there. Squads or towers in range can clear a den. Clearing one gives your team Sugar High (units 25% faster and 40% stronger for 45s), 150 energy for each teammate, and a Builder super: Rock Candy Golem, Sugar Storm or Sprinkle Frenzy.
  • After your first stump link, the piece queue adds Hot Sauce (burns invaders on your road) and Gum (slows them). Commanders can drop a Lemon Wall on an enemy road: a tough barricade their squads must smash through.
  • Units only ever walk on roads. If a road breaks, they pile up at the gap until someone repairs it (donuts love a crowd). Once you topple a section's Grumpstump, your Builder can lay roads on the enemy half of that section at double cost to push toward their spire, keep and castle.
  • Delete removes a road piece, but only if no link breaks. On empty ground in Road mode, it throws away the current piece for 3E.

Old Grumpstumps

One grumpy tree stump guards each section and shoots both teams. Units cannot pass until it falls.

  • The team that lands the final hit gets 120 energy for each of its players.
  • Every lane ends at a stump. A road that gets within 3 tiles of a stump snaps onto it. When a stump falls, rubble road paves itself from the stump to both castles, so the lane always connects end to end.
  • Lost socks, coin jars and gift boxes sit on the map. Lay a road over one to give your Commander energy or your Builder a card.
  • Once a stump falls, attackers march toward the enemy castle, preferring roads. That means your own roads lead the enemy home, so line them with towers.

Cards

Your towers sometimes drop a card when they get a kill. The shop has 3 offers and restocks every 30s. Highlights include Banana Peel, Group Chat Drama, Snack Diplomacy and Oops All Nuke, which also hits your own stuff.

Hover for stats

Hover the mouse over any unit, tower, stump, castle, road, card or menu icon to see its cost, damage, and what it is strong and weak against. On a keyboard or gamepad, rest your cursor on something for a moment. Newly selected items also show their stats above your panel.

Online play

Click Play online on the title screen. One person hosts and shares the 4-letter code or invite link, and friends join and pick a slot. Empty slots are played by the CPU.

Online, everyone uses the same controls on their own computer: WASD or arrows to move, Q to cycle (Shift+Q goes back), mouse wheel to zoom, click-drag to pan, E or Space to use, Z or Tab to change mode, X or Del to delete, and Enter to chat. The mouse works too. You only see your own team's panels, pings, doodles and chat.
